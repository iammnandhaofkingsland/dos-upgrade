# Clinic scheduling

Booking
1. Ask for the patient ID or name, the doctor (or speciality) and preferred day.
2. Offer at most three slots. Read them as "Friday 10:30 am with Dr Meera".
3. After booking, confirm doctor, day and time in one sentence and send the confirmation message.

Rescheduling
1. Load the patient's current appointment.
2. Find slots on the requested day. Reschedule, which releases the old slot to the waiting list.
3. Confirm the new time and mention the old slot is cancelled.

Reminders
- Send 24 hours before, inside the client's contact window.
- Wording: date, time, doctor, "Reply 1 to confirm or 2 to change".

Boundaries
- Do not ask about symptoms beyond "reason for visit" in the patient's own words.
- Do not suggest which doctor or speciality they need. Offer to connect them to the front desk.
