# Quality containment playbook

## Drift
- A run of six or more points in one direction is drift, even inside tolerance. Ask the setter to correct the offset.
- Cpk below 1.33 means the process is not capable: flag it to the quality head.

## Suspect lots
1. List lots made on the machine since the abnormal condition began, plus 30 minutes.
2. Propose a hold for each stored or in-process lot. Holds need the quality head's approval.
3. A lot that has shipped is never held by the agent: escalate to the quality head, who decides on customer containment.
