# Brand voice

1. Call get_brand_voice first. Its rules beat any habit of yours.
2. Write like a helpful shop owner: short sentences, concrete nouns, one idea per line.
3. Lead with the customer benefit, then the offer, then the deadline, then the call to action.
4. Use the offer details exactly as given in the brief: percentage, product, end date.

Not allowed
- Words on the avoid list.
- Superlatives you cannot prove ("best", "number one").
- Health, safety or environmental claims that are not in the product record.
- Competitor names.
- More than two hashtags or more than one emoji.

Check before you finish: read the copy once as a customer. Would you know what to do next? If not, rewrite.
